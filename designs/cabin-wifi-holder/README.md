# Cabin Wi-Fi QR + IR remote holder

Wall-mounted holder for a small IR remote (43 x 90 x 5 mm). The Wi-Fi QR is on the front panel of the pocket. White body, raised black QR, Wi-Fi glyph and `Si_Cabin`. Full spec: [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).

**Wi-Fi credentials:** `tools/qr.js`, `qr_matrix.scad`, `WiFi_QR_test.png` and `print/` hold the password (directly or as a QR), so they are git-ignored and exist only on the local machine. Never commit them. The `.scad` needs `qr_matrix.scad` (generated by `tools/qr.js`) to build.

Not physically tested yet: remote fit, glue, covers and iPhone QR scan are still to do.

## Files

- `cabin-wifi-holder.scad`: parametric source. `part` = `all`, `A`, `B`, `BC`, `C`, `D`. Uses `qr_matrix.scad` (generated by `tools/qr.js`).
- `print/cabin-wifi-holder-plate1.3mf`: Bambu Studio project (two plates, all four parts). STLs are not stored: rebuild with `build.sh` using `part` = `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`.

## Print

Bambu Lab A1 + AMS Lite, 0.4 mm nozzle, `0.20mm Standard @BBL A1`, no supports, default profile. Filament 1 white, filament 2 black (project slots currently read "PLA Matte": make sure they match the loaded spools).

| Plate | Contents | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 "Remote Holder" | A + 4 covers D (2 spare), white | 28m33s |
| 2 "QR Code" | B + C, one colour swap | 24m8s |

Total 52m42s, 23.34 g (white 22.30 g, black 1.04 g), from the slice before the Hello Additive pass. Hello Additive (Bambu's AI print optimiser) was then run and added about 24 s; the saved 3mf includes it. Run it on every custom model before queueing.

## Assembly

1. Print A, B+C, D. Check the remote drops into A's pocket.
2. Dry-fit B on A, line up the bottom and side edges, tape.
3. Small beads of gel superglue on the outer half of the wall tops and floor top, never at the pocket edge.
4. Screw A to the wall with two ~20 mm countersunk screws (lift the remote out first), push in covers D. Pry out at the notch.
5. Test the QR with an iPhone before mounting.
